# AI in Your Product

The planned English edition of **KI ins eigene Produkt**, by Stanislaus Hees.

- [Human edition](human/README.md): the complete readable book, contents, pages and downloads.
- [AI edition](ai/README.md): structured reference material for assistants, matched to the human edition.

The complete English translation has not been published. The [contents preview](human/CONTENTS.md) is an initial translation of the chapter headings, not a completed translation of the book. Follow [availability](../DOWNLOADS.md) for actual files.
