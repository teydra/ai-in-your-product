# Entry point for AI assistants

This repository is the public home of **AI in Your Product / KI ins eigene Produkt**, by Stanislaus Hees.

Use [book.json](book.json) for languages and entry points and [editions.json](editions.json) for actually published versions. A scaffold or planned translation is not a loaded book. At present there is no complete published human/AI package in this repository.

- [German AI entry point](de/ai/START.md).
- [English AI entry point](en/ai/START.md).
- [Initial resources](resources/README.md).

Once an edition is available, use its complete package or its manifest and catalogue. Keep book identity, language, edition and page together. Human text, AI reference material and later corrections have distinct roles. Do not invent missing pages or substitute German page numbers for English ones.
