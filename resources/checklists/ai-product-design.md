# AI product design checklist

- Can you describe one useful task in the product, rather than only naming a model?
- Does the AI receive the information needed for the current decision, or have a way to obtain it?
- Are available tools described in terms of what they actually do, their inputs and their results?
- Does the application enforce access, state and effects that must not depend on a model's wording?
- Does the result of each action become information for the next step?
- Have you followed an ordinary example and a failure case through the whole system?
- Can you identify what the model may decide and what the software must guarantee?

Use these questions on your own implementation. They are an initial companion resource, not a certification or a claim that the complete book has been reviewed.
