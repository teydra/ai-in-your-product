# Product brief

## The product and the task

What does the product do today? Which specific task should AI take on? What outcome would help the person using it?

## Available information

Which information is already known, which can be retrieved, and which is missing? What must be current at the moment a decision is made?

## Available capabilities

What can the software actually read or change? Which actions require a person's approval? What must be enforced by the application?

## Results and next steps

How will the result of each action return to the AI? How can the AI recognize success, failure or missing information?

## A concrete example

Write one normal case and one case where the needed information is absent or a tool fails. Follow both through the proposed system.
