# Examine my AI product

Copy the following into your assistant and fill in the product description. It is a starting prompt, not a substitute for the complete AI edition.

> Help me understand how AI should work inside my product. My product is: [describe it]. The task I want AI to take on is: [describe one concrete task].
>
> First separate what is known about my actual software from assumptions and questions. Work through a concrete example: what information the AI needs, how it can obtain it, what capabilities the software exposes, what the software must guarantee, and how results return for the next decision. Explain unfamiliar terms when they become necessary. Ask for missing facts instead of inventing existing integrations. Start with analysis; do not change files or run actions without my instruction.
