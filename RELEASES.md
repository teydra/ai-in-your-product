# Published editions

No complete book edition has been published here yet. The current repository contains the selected German cover, the four edition areas and initial companion resources.

Each future release will identify its language, edition, matching human and AI files, page count, contents, download links and SHA256 checksums. Every human page will be included, rather than selected samples only. Changes to printed text or pagination receive a new edition; existing page references remain attached to their original files.

English and German editions are tracked separately. An AI update can accompany the same unchanged human edition when the manifest records that relationship explicitly.
